Discover High Point, NC: The East Coast’s First Certified Autism Destination™

A practical starting point for planning a more sensory-aware North Carolina getaway.

Planning a vacation when someone in your family is autistic or has sensory sensitivities can bring extra uncertainty. Busy public spaces, unexpected sounds, and unfamiliar routines may make a getaway feel harder to plan.

High Point, North Carolina, has been recognized as the East Coast’s first Certified Autism Destination™. Through a community-wide effort with the International Board of Credentialing and Continuing Education Standards (IBCCES), participating local organizations have pursued autism and sensory-awareness training. A destination designation can be a helpful starting point, while individual services and supports may vary by venue.

Sensory-aware places to explore

Nido & Mariana Qubein Children’s Museum

This interactive museum can be a fun family stop. Before your visit, check the museum’s current visitor information for exhibit details, sensory resources, quiet spaces, and availability of items such as sensory bags or headphones. Experiences and accommodations may change, so contacting the museum ahead of time can help you plan for your family’s needs.

High Point Public Library

The library may offer a quieter break from a busy itinerary. Contact the library to ask about current quiet-room access, sensory supports, and inclusive programs before relying on a specific accommodation during your visit.

Choose a comfortable home base

When booking lodging or dining, ask specific questions rather than assuming every business offers the same supports. You might request a room away from elevators, ask about quieter dining times, and confirm options for dietary needs. A little advance planning can make it easier to build in downtime.

Make the trip work for your family

  • Review venue information and photos before you go, and contact staff with questions about sensory supports.
  • Plan breaks and a quieter alternative in case a space feels overwhelming.
  • Keep familiar comfort items and essentials easy to access.
  • Confirm hours, availability, and accommodations directly with each venue shortly before your trip.

High Point can be a promising option for families looking for a more inclusive travel experience. Every traveler is different, so checking details in advance helps you decide what will feel comfortable and manageable for your family.
