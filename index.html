About Our Agency

Meet the team dedicated to your family's travel comfort

Hi, I’m Jason Reeves

Alongside my wife Marsha, I run Alpine Tide Travel out of Dallas, North Carolina. Travel has the power to restore and inspire us, but we also know that traditional travel environments can introduce sensory overload, stress, and unpredictable barriers for neurodivergent individuals and families handling autism or special accommodations.

I have an Adult Diagnosis of Autism and I want to use that to help others navigate the World. That is why we have engineered our agency format to specialize directly in autism-friendly and sensory-aware vacation engineering. Whether you are seeking a quiet mountain getaway, a slow-paced beach retreat, or an accessible cruise ship layout, we advocate on your behalf. We match your specific environmental preferences with verified, certified properties and cruising options across our global WorldVia network.

Technical Credentials

Our personal dedication is backed by industry-standard training, including Carnival Cruise Line Diamond Level Expert and Princess Cruises Commodore distinctions. We combine high-level technical booking knowledge with specialized protocols to support families who need a calm, structured, and completely reliable planning interface.
