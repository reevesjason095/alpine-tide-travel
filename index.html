We will be heading to Florida on August 22 to take a 7-night cruise for our 21st Anniversary, but our travel focus is now expanding to include more land travel, resort stays, and scenic getaways too.

The first stop on our journey in this blog will be Turks and Caicos.

Here are three fantastic ways to maximize your time porting in Turks and Caicos (Grand Turk):

  • Relax at Governor's Beach: Located within Columbus Landfall National Park, this stunning white-sand beach is one of the most beautiful spots on the island. It sits just a short ride from the cruise port by taxi or rental golf cart.
  • Snorkel with Stingrays at Gibbs Cay: For an unforgettable marine adventure, book a boat excursion to Gibbs Cay. This small, uninhabited island features crystal-clear shallows where visitors can swim and interact with gentle, wild stingrays.
  • Explore via Golf Cart Rental: Renting a golf cart is the ultimate way to see Grand Turk at your own pace. It gives you the freedom to cruise up to Cockburn Town, visit historic lighthouses, and find secluded beaches away from the typical tourist crowds.

Thank you for reading! Be sure to check back soon, as we will be updating the site with more destination guides and travel tips for both cruise and land travel.

San Juan, Puerto Rico

Next up on this itinerary is San Juan, Puerto Rico, a vibrant port known for its colorful colonial architecture, massive fortresses, and rich culture. When your cruise ship docks, you will be steps away from centuries of history.

Here are the top ways to spend your day:

  • Step Back in Time in Old San Juan: The cruise piers sit right at the edge of the historic district. Spend your morning wandering the cobblestone streets, admiring the pastel-colored buildings, and visiting the iconic 16th-century fortress, Castillo San Felipe del Morro (El Morro).
  • Hike through El Yunque National Forest: If you prefer nature over history, book an excursion to the only tropical rainforest in the U.S. National Forest System. It features lush hiking trails, incredible biodiversity, and scenic natural waterfalls perfect for a refreshing swim.
  • Sip an Authentic Piña Colada: Puerto Rico is the birthplace of this famous tropical drink. Take a break from exploring to grab lunch at a local café, order some traditional mofongo (mashed plantains), and enjoy a fresh cocktail right where it was invented.

Amber Cove, Dominican Republic

Located on the northern "Amber Coast" of the Dominican Republic, Amber Cove is a modern, resort-style port designed specifically for cruise passengers. It serves as both a relaxing sanctuary and a gateway to outdoor adventure.

Here is how to make the most of your visit:

  • Lounge at the Aqua Zone: You do not even need to leave the port to have a fantastic day. Amber Cove features a massive, resort-style pool complex that is completely free to access. It includes a swim-up bar, waterslides, plenty of lounge chairs, and over-water cabanas available for rent.
  • Chasing Waterfalls at Damajagua: For thrill-seekers, the 27 Waterfalls of Damajagua is a must-do excursion. Guided tours take you deep into the jungle to hike up, then slide, jump, and swim down a series of spectacular limestone canyons and natural waterslides.
  • Meet the Locals at Monkeyland: Take an open-air safari truck into the Dominican countryside to visit a beautiful monkey sanctuary. Here, playful squirrel monkeys interact directly with visitors, climbing right onto your shoulders for unforgettable photos.
