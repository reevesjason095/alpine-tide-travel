The Greenbrier Bunker

The Insider Guide to Touring the Greenbrier Bunker: Can You Go Alone?

Built in the 1950s with the code name "Project Greek Island," the Greenbrier Bunker served as a top-secret relocation facility for the United States Congress.

It was fortified with a 25-ton blast door, decontamination chambers, and independent water and power supplies. Today, it is open to the public for guided tours.

Do You Need a Group to Tour the Greenbrier Bunker?

Clear answer first: No, individuals do not need to bring their own group to tour the bunker.

Essential Bunker Tour Rules to Know Before You Go:

  • Reservations are mandatory: Book in advance through the official Greenbrier Bunker Tours portal.
  • Age Limits: The minimum age requirement is 10 years old.
  • No-Electronics Policy: Cameras, phones, smartwatches, and recording equipment are not allowed.
